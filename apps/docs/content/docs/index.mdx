## Know what is available

<Callout title="Implemented workflows and future direction">
Next.js and Laravel generation, editing, isolated previews and publishing are implemented.
Generated-app databases are in development. Vue, Django and Figma connectors are planned.
[Explore capabilities and the roadmap](/docs/roadmap).
</Callout>

A revision is a saved version of your generated app. When you ask for a change, Wrenly
uses the selected revision as its starting point. Your earlier versions remain available.
[Learn about conversations and revisions](/docs/architecture/conversations).
